---
title: Voice room audio quality is poor
category: Troubleshooting
last_updated: 2026-05-21
---

Poor voice room quality is usually caused by network conditions or microphone settings. Try these steps in order:

1. **Check your connection** — run a speed test. Voice rooms need at least 1 Mbps upload.
2. **Close other tabs** that use audio or video (video calls, music streaming).
3. **Select your microphone** explicitly in the voice room settings — browser default may pick the wrong device.
4. **Use Chrome or Edge** — Safari has known WebRTC limitations that affect audio quality.
5. **Reload the page** — a stale WebRTC connection can degrade over time.

If the problem persists after these steps, contact us with your browser version and rough geographic location.
