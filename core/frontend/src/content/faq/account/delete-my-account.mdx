---
title: How do I delete my account?
category: Account
last_updated: 2026-05-21
---

You can request account deletion from **Settings → Account → Delete account**. This action is irreversible and will permanently remove your profile, conversations, projects, and billing data.

If you have an active subscription, cancel it first to avoid further charges. Refunds for unused periods are handled case-by-case.

Your deletion request is processed within 30 days in accordance with GDPR Article 17. You'll receive a confirmation email when the deletion is complete.
