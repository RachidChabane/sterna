---
title: I forgot my password
category: Account
last_updated: 2026-05-21
---

Go to the login page and click **Forgot password?** Enter your email address and we'll send a reset link. The link is valid for 1 hour.

If you signed up with GitHub OAuth you don't have a password — use the **Continue with GitHub** button instead.

If you're not receiving the reset email, check your spam folder or contact us with your account email and we'll sort it out.
