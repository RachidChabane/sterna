---
title: My email isn't verified — what do I do?
category: Account
last_updated: 2026-05-21
---

Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a verification email from `noreply@example.com`. Click the link inside to verify your address.

If you didn't receive it, go to **Settings → Account → Resend verification email**. The link expires after 24 hours, so request a fresh one if yours is older than that.

If you signed up with GitHub OAuth, your email is verified automatically through GitHub's verified email address.
