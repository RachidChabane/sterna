---
title: How do I cancel my subscription?
category: Billing
last_updated: 2026-05-21
---

You can cancel your subscription at any time from **Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

After cancellation you'll be downgraded to the free tier. Your conversations, projects, and data are retained — nothing is deleted automatically.

If you're having trouble finding the cancel button, reach out via the Contact tab and we'll take care of it promptly.
