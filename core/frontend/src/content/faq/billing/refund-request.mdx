---
title: How do I request a refund?
category: Billing
last_updated: 2026-05-21
---

We offer refunds within 7 days of a charge if you haven't used the plan features. Contact us through the Contact tab with the subject "Refund request" and include your account email and the charge date.

Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days back to your original payment method.

Usage-based charges (AI tokens, coding agent sessions) are generally non-refundable once consumed, but we'll review edge cases on a case-by-case basis.
