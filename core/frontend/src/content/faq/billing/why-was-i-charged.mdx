---
title: Why was I charged?
category: Billing
last_updated: 2026-05-21
---

Sterna charges based on your subscription plan plus any usage that exceeds your plan's included quota. Charges appear on the billing date shown in your account settings.

If you see an unexpected charge, check your **Usage** page for a breakdown by service and date. Each AI model call, coding agent session, and voice room minute is tracked separately.

If something still looks wrong after reviewing your usage, contact us through the Contact tab and include the charge date and amount.
