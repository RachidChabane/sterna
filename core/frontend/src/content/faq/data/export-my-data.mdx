---
title: How do I export my data?
category: Data & Privacy
last_updated: 2026-05-21
---

You can export all your data from **Settings → Privacy → Export my data**. The export includes:

- All conversations and messages
- Knowledge base documents
- Project files and coding agent outputs
- Account and billing history

The export is prepared as a ZIP file and emailed to your account email within 24 hours. Large accounts may take longer.

This feature is provided in accordance with GDPR Article 20 (right to data portability).
