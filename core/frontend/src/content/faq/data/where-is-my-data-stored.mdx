---
title: Where is my data stored?
category: Data & Privacy
last_updated: 2026-05-21
---

Sterna stores your data in the EU (AWS eu-west-1 for application data, Neon PostgreSQL for the database). We do not transfer personal data outside the EU/EEA without appropriate safeguards.

**What we store:**
- Conversation messages and metadata
- Knowledge base documents you upload
- Sandbox workspace files (temporary, 7-day retention after last access)
- Billing and usage records

AI model providers (via OpenRouter) process your prompts in accordance with their own data processing agreements. We recommend reviewing OpenRouter's privacy policy for model-specific data handling.

For questions about data residency or DPA agreements, contact us via the Contact tab.
