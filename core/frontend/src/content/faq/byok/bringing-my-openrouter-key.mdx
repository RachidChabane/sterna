---
title: How do I bring my own OpenRouter key?
category: BYOK
last_updated: 2026-05-21
---

Sterna supports Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) via OpenRouter. Go to **Settings → API Keys → OpenRouter** and paste your key. Your key is encrypted at rest and never logged.

When a BYOK key is active, model calls are billed directly to your OpenRouter account instead of your Sterna balance. This is ideal for heavy users who already have OpenRouter credits.

You can remove or rotate your key at any time from the same settings page. Removing it reverts to Sterna-managed routing.
