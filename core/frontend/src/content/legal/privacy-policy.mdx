## Categories of personal data we process

When you use the Service we collect:

- **Account data** — email address; full name; password hash (when you sign up with email); OAuth profile data (name, avatar URL, provider user ID) when you sign in with Google or GitHub.
- **Chat content** — the prompts you send, files you upload, and the AI responses generated for you. This includes messages exchanged in chats, voice rooms, and image / video generation sessions.
- **Billing metadata** — payment status and subscription level from Stripe. We do not receive your full card number; Stripe holds that data.
- **Connection data** — IP address, browser user-agent, approximate location derived from IP (country / region only), and the rough timestamps of your sessions. We use this for rate limiting, abuse detection, and security forensics.
- **Device fingerprint** — a coarse hash of browser characteristics used solely to detect account-sharing abuse.
- **Diagnostic data** — application logs containing request IDs, error stacks, and feature flags. We strip personal identifiers from these logs before persistence where feasible.

## Why we process your data (purposes)

We process the data above to:

1. Deliver the Service to you (run chats, render outputs, store your work).
2. Bill your subscription and prevent payment fraud.
3. Keep the Service secure (rate-limit abuse, detect compromised accounts, comply with takedown requests).
4. Improve the product through aggregated analytics (which features are used, where users encounter errors).
5. Communicate with you about account activity, security alerts, and (with your consent) product news.

## International transfers

When your data is sent to a sub-processor in a country outside the European Economic Area, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) published by the European Commission, supplemented by encryption in transit and at rest. Where the recipient is in a country covered by an EU adequacy decision (e.g. the United Kingdom), we rely on that decision.

The SCC text is available at <https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/standard-contractual-clauses-scc_en>.

## Retention periods

- **Active account data** — for as long as your account is open.
- **Deleted account** — 30-day soft-delete window for recovery, then we hard-purge identifying data.
- **Chat history** — until you delete it or until your account is hard-deleted.
- **Server logs** — 90 days, rolling.
- **Stripe billing records** — 10 years to satisfy tax and accounting obligations.
- **Security incident records** — up to 2 years after closure of the incident.

## Your rights (GDPR Art. 15–22)

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data (Art. 15).
- **Rectify** inaccurate data (Art. 16).
- **Erase** your data (Art. 17), subject to overriding legal obligations such as billing records.
- **Restrict** processing (Art. 18).
- **Port** your data to another provider (Art. 20).
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).
- **Withdraw consent** at any time for processing that relies on your consent.

To exercise any of these, go to Settings → Account → Data, or email `privacy@example.com`. We respond within 30 days.
