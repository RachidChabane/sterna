This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") lists the things you must not do with Sterna. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms); a violation can lead to suspension or termination of your account.

## Prohibited content

You must not use the Service to produce, request, store, or distribute:

- **Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)** or any sexual content involving minors. We have zero tolerance for this category. We ban the account immediately, preserve the evidence, and report to the lawful authorities (e.g. NCMEC in the United States, CSAM-tipline.eu in Europe).
- **Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII)** — sexual images of identifiable adults without their consent, including deepfakes.
- **Terrorism content** as defined by EU Regulation 2021/784, including material that incites, solicits, or facilitates terrorist acts.
- **Hate speech** that targets people based on a protected characteristic (race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, age).
- **Harassment, doxxing, or threats** directed at specific individuals.
- **Malware, exploit code, or phishing content** intended to harm computer systems or steal credentials.

## Prohibited conduct

You must not:

- Scrape the Service or its outputs at industrial scale; no scraping for resale or for training third-party models.
- Use credential-stuffing, brute-force, or automated account-creation tools.
- Evade rate limits or abuse the free tier (e.g. through multiple shadow accounts).
- Reverse engineer, decompile, or otherwise attempt to extract source code, model weights, or non-public APIs.
- Resell or repackage the Service without a written agreement from Sterna.

## Generative AI restrictions

In addition to the above, you must not:

- Use model outputs to impersonate real people without their explicit consent (voice clones, photorealistic deepfakes, fabricated quotes).
- Run coordinated disinformation campaigns, including the mass generation of fake reviews, fake user personas, or political propaganda.
- Submit jailbreak prompts intended to evade the safety policies of the underlying models.

## Enforcement

We apply graduated enforcement: warning → account suspension → termination. For severe categories (CSAM, NCII, terrorism, doxxing, security attacks on the Service itself) we skip directly to termination and may report the matter to law enforcement. We may also preserve evidence to support criminal investigations.

If you believe an enforcement action was issued in error, reply to the enforcement email with an explanation; we re-review within 14 days.
