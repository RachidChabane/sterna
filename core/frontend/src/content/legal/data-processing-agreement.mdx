This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between Sterna ("Processor") and you, the Customer ("Controller") when Customer Personal Data is processed on the Sterna Service. It is offered for B2B customers who require a separate DPA to meet their own GDPR obligations.

## Definitions

The terms **Controller**, **Processor**, **Personal Data**, **Data Subject**, and **Sub-processor** have the meanings given in Article 4 of the GDPR. **Customer Personal Data** is the Personal Data that Controller submits to the Service.

## Subject matter and duration of processing

The subject matter is the provision of the Sterna AI platform to the Controller. The duration is the term of the main Sterna agreement and, where applicable, the deletion-or-return period defined in §12.

## Nature and purpose

Processor provides an AI assistant service: it hosts Customer Personal Data, transmits it to the underlying model providers needed to fulfil Controller's request, stores the outputs returned, and retains audit logs.

## Categories of data subjects

The Data Subjects are typically Controller's authorized end users (employees, contractors, or other named individuals).

## Categories of personal data

The categories of Personal Data are those described in §2 of the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). For B2B deployments, Controller is responsible for ensuring no special-category data (Art. 9 GDPR) is submitted unless an additional written annex specifies otherwise.

## Controller and Processor obligations

- Processor processes Customer Personal Data only on the documented instructions of Controller, except where required by EU or Member State law.
- Processor ensures that persons authorized to process Customer Personal Data have committed to confidentiality.
- Processor implements appropriate technical and organizational measures (encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, vulnerability management) as described in the security overview shared on request.
- Processor assists Controller with data-subject requests and with the Controller's own Art. 32–36 GDPR obligations.

## Sub-processors

Processor's current Sub-processors are listed in §5 of the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). Controller hereby gives general written authorization for Processor to engage these Sub-processors. Processor will notify Controller of any new Sub-processor at least 30 days before it begins processing. Controller may object on reasonable grounds within that window; if Processor cannot accommodate the objection, Controller may terminate the affected portion of the Service.

## International transfers

Where Customer Personal Data is transferred outside the EEA, the Parties incorporate by reference the Standard Contractual Clauses, Module Two (Controller-to-Processor), as published by the European Commission. The SCC text is available at <https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/standard-contractual-clauses-scc_en>.

## Audits

Processor will make available to Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR. On 30 days' written notice, no more than once per year (or as required by a supervisory authority), Controller may request an audit conducted by a mutually agreed independent auditor at Controller's cost, subject to reasonable confidentiality and operational safeguards.

## Data subject requests

If Processor receives a request from a Data Subject relating to Customer Personal Data, Processor will not respond to the Data Subject directly without Controller's prior authorization, except where required by law. Processor assists Controller in responding within the statutory deadlines.

## Breach notification

Processor will notify Controller without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data. The notification will include the information required by Article 33(3) GDPR to the extent known.

## Deletion or return of data

At the end of the provision of services, Processor will, at the choice of Controller, delete or return all Customer Personal Data, and delete existing copies, unless EU or Member State law requires further storage.

## Liability

The liability provisions of the main Sterna agreement apply to this DPA.
