This Cookie Policy explains how Sterna uses cookies and similar technologies (localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB) when you visit our site or use the Service.

## What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They let the site remember things across page loads, such as that you are logged in. We also use similar browser-side storage: **localStorage** for preferences and **IndexedDB** for cached attachments. Throughout this policy we use "cookies" loosely to cover all of these.

## Categories we use

We group our use into three categories.

- **Strictly necessary** — required for the Service to work at all. They cannot be switched off. Examples: the JWT access token and refresh token that keep you signed in, and the CSRF token used to protect forms.
- **Functional** — improve your experience but are not strictly necessary. Examples: theme preference (light or dark), sidebar collapse state, last-viewed conversation. You can clear these by signing out, which removes the user-scoped storage keys.
- **Analytics** — measure how the Service is used so we can improve it. These are only set if you opt in via the consent banner. ## How to manage cookies

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings:

- **Chrome / Edge** — Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data.
- **Firefox** — Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
- **Safari** — Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data.

If you opt out of analytics cookies through our in-app consent banner (when published), we stop setting those keys.
