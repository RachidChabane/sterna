This Refund Policy explains when you can get your money back, how to ask, and how long it takes.

## 14-day cooling-off period (EU consumers)

Under EU Directive 2011/83/EU on consumer rights, if you are a consumer resident in the European Union you have a 14-day cooling-off period from the day you subscribe.

- If you did **not** use the Service during the cooling-off period, you are entitled to a full refund on request.
- If you **did** use the Service during the cooling-off period, we calculate a pro-rated refund based on the proportion of the billing cycle remaining when you request the refund, less any one-off usage costs (image / video generation, sandbox compute) that have already settled. Email `billing@example.com` from the email address on your account, mentioning "Cooling-off refund". We process the refund within 14 days of receiving your request.

## Annual subscriptions

Outside the EU cooling-off window, annual subscriptions are non-refundable once the billing cycle has started. If you switch plans mid-cycle we apply a pro-rated credit toward your new plan.

## Monthly subscriptions

Outside the EU cooling-off window, monthly subscriptions are non-refundable. Cancellation is effective at the end of the current month; you keep access until then and we do not bill the next cycle.

## Disputed charges

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact us first at `billing@example.com` so we can investigate. If the dispute is not resolved, you may raise it with Stripe directly via the dispute process described in <https://stripe.com/docs/disputes>.

## How to request a refund

You can request a refund in two ways:

- **In-app**: Settings → Billing → Request refund.
- **Email**: `billing@example.com` from the account email.

Refunds are issued back to the original payment method. Stripe typically completes the refund within 5–10 business days; the exact timing depends on your card issuer.
